    Safety

    Your keys never leave your computer.

    Noctenna runs on your computer. It can prepare anything, and only you can send it.

    • A wallet in your keychain

      No server ever sees a key, and no tool can read one.

    • Your yes for every trade

      It prepares and shows the summary. Only your yes sends it.

    • Exact approvals, daily limit

      Never unlimited allowances. You set the dollar limit.

    • No shell, no files

      Typed chain tools only. Token text is read as data, never as orders.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Short answers. The README on GitHub has the long ones.

    Is it free?

    Yes. Noctenna is open source under the MIT licence and adds no fee to any swap, trade or bridge. You pay your own AI model and normal network fees.

    Where are my keys?

    In your computer's keychain. No server ever sees them, no tool can read them, and exporting a key only works in your own terminal.

    Can it trade without asking me?

    No. It prepares every action and shows you the summary first. Nothing is sent until you say yes, and a daily dollar limit you set caps what it can spend.

    Which AI models work?

    Any key from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or OpenRouter, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or a local model through Ollama. You can also plug the same tools into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or Gemini CLI.

    Is trading live?

    Not yet on mainnet. Everything that reads is live now: launch checks, funding, perps markets, balances and quotes. Trading opens at the token launch, and testnet is open for practice.

    Can I use perps where I live?

    Arcus and Lighter are not available in the US, Canada, the UK or sanctioned regions. Noctenna reminds you before the first perps action. Use them only where you may.
